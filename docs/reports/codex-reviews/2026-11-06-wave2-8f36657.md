1. **P1 - Stapled SCTs incorrectly count retired logs.** [ct.rs:469](F:/Projects/Code/Sieve/.claude/worktrees/wave-2/engine/crates/sieve-engine/src/tls/ct.rs:469) applies the embedded-log retirement allowance to stapled SCTs too. A certificate without embedded SCTs, accompanied by two valid stapled SCTs from different operators-one live log and one retired log-returns `Compliant` when their earliest timestamp predates retirement. Sieve can then mint a trusted replacement certificate despite the upstream failing Chrome’s policy. Even Chrome’s older policy accepting OCSP SCTs required both qualifying logs to be live; Chrome 148 onward excludes OCSP SCTs altogether. [Chrome CT policy](https://googlechrome.github.io/CertificateTransparency/ct_policy.html)

2. **P1 - Ignoring operator history can falsely establish diversity.** [ct.rs:346](F:/Projects/Code/Sieve/.claude/worktrees/wave-2/engine/crates/sieve-engine/src/tls/ct.rs:346) assigns every SCT its log’s current operator. Suppose two logs issued a short-lived certificate’s SCTs while both belonged to operator A, then one transferred to B. A list containing `previous_operators` produces two counted operators here, allowing interception; Chrome counts ownership at issuance and rejects that quorum. [Chrome CT policy](https://googlechrome.github.io/CertificateTransparency/ct_policy.html) The claim at `docs/tasks/2026-11-06-validation.md:78` that this omission only causes pass-through is **reported, not observed**: no operator-history probe accompanies it, and this scenario produces the opposite result.

3. **P1 - Interception starts before validation data finishes loading.** [routing.rs:347](F:/Projects/Code/Sieve/.claude/worktrees/wave-2/engine/crates/sieve-engine/src/control/routing.rs:347) spawns the reload without awaiting it, then publishes interception immediately. On startup with valid validation files present but loading delayed, a connection can see the initially empty slot, skip CT/CRLite, pass platform trust, and receive a minted certificate. Completing the reload does not revalidate that existing connection. Initial loading must finish before interception becomes available.

4. **P2 - The ECH cache extends authoritative TTLs and can trigger plaintext fallback.** [ech.rs:80](F:/Projects/Code/Sieve/.claude/worktrees/wave-2/engine/crates/sieve-engine/src/tls/ech.rs:80) raises a one-second TTL to 60 seconds. The adjacent `ECH_LOOKUP_WAIT=300ms`, `ECH_HANDSHAKE_TIMEOUT=3s`, `MAX_TTL_S=3600`, `NEGATIVE_TTL_MS=300000`, and `FAILED_TTL_MS=30000` likewise have descriptions but no derivation from a stated law.
